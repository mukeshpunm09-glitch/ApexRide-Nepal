About ApexRide Nepal

Our Story

ApexRide Nepal began with a simple frustration: renting a good vehicle in Nepal was slow, uncertain, and priced in whispers.

In 2025, Mr. Bikalpa Khanal needed a reliable SUV for a family trip from Ghorahi to Pokhara — and spent two days calling strangers, haggling over prices, and worrying whether the car would even arrive. His friend Madhu Pun had the same story with a bike in Kathmandu. Together, they decided Nepal deserved better.

They built ApexRide Nepal as a platform where every owner is verified with Nagarikta / National ID and Bluebook documents, every renter is verified with a driving licence and selfie, and every price is fixed and visible in Nepalese Rupees — Rs. 500 for a standard cycle up to Rs. 15,000 for a luxury car. No haggling. No surprises.

Today ApexRide serves Kathmandu, Pokhara, Ghorahi and beyond — with 24/7 road assistance, transparent rental agreements, and a team that answers the phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Customers need a Nagarikta (Citizenship) Number OR National ID Number, a valid Driving Licence Number with a clear licence photo, and a live/recent full-face selfie for verification.

Owners need BOTH Nagarikta AND National ID, a selfie, a Bluebook / vehicle registration document, and a recent 360° walkaround video of the vehicle.

Fixed prices (Rs./day): Standard Cycle 500 · Luxury Cycle 2,000 · Standard Bike 2,000 · Luxury Bike 7,000 · Standard Car 7,000 · Luxury Car 15,000. Plus 5% service fee and 13% VAT, shown before you pay.

Return with the same fuel level (fuel replacement rule), pass a return inspection, and your refundable security deposit is returned in full. Verified damage is deducted from the deposit only.
